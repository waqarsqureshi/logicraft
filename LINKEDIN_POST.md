# Announcing LogiCraft: Zero-Friction Digital Logic Circuit Simulator 🚀

Most digital logic simulators out there come with a steep learning curve. Students often get bogged down by clumsy pick-and-place canvas controls, cryptic VHDL/Verilog syntax, or outdated desktop software before they can even grasp basic Boolean logic.

Our mission with **LogiCraft** is different: **Our goal is not to write synthesis-grade VHDL code, but to teach students how digital logic truly works for a deep, intuitive understanding of computer architecture and modern computing systems.**

✨ **Why Educators & Students Love LogiCraft:**
- **Zero-Friction Pick & Place**: Drag-and-drop gates, multiplexers, latches, flip-flops, adders, and 7-segment displays directly in your browser with zero installation.
- **Orthogonal Square-Corner Wire Routing**: Click any point on the canvas to place clean 90° bends and square feedback loops (e.g. from Q̄ to D on flip-flops).
- **Sequential Circuits & Real-Time Probes**: Interactive 2-bit & 4-bit ripple counters, synchronous binary counters, and 4-bit SIPO/PISO shift registers.
- **Transistor-Level Insights**: Step inside standard CMOS gates (AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR) to see NMOS and PMOS silicon channel conduction in real time.
- **Built-in Truth Tables & Timing Analyzers**: Live waveform plotting, propagation delay visualization, and automatic Boolean expression verification.
- **Mobile & Touch-Optimized**: Full pinch-to-zoom and two-finger pan support for iPads, Chromebooks, and smartphones.
